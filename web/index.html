The client signed off the design. When does the RM18,000 arrive?设计稿客户已经确认了，RM18,000 什么时候能到账？

SDC Tracker turns a finished milestone into an approved invoice, helps you follow up when it runs late, and shows what a late payment does to the next 30 days of cash.SDC Tracker 把做完的阶段变成一张经过审批的发票，逾期了帮你准备好提醒，再告诉你这笔钱晚到会让未来 30 天的现金变成什么样。

  • Nothing is invoiced or sent until a person approves it开票和发送提醒之前，一定有人确认过
  • Clicking twice never creates a second invoice or reminder点两次也只会开一张发票、发一次提醒
  • Every number traces back to a line you can check每个数字都能在收支明细里对上
